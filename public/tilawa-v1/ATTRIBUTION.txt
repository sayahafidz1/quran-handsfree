According to the Tilawa model card, the models and the reference data are built from these works,
whose attribution terms also apply to redistribution of the models and assets:

- tlog (tarteel-ai/tlog), by Tarteel, CC-BY: training and evaluation audio.
- Quranic Universal Ayahs and the QUD alignment benchmark (QUD-Technologies/quranic-universal-ayahs),
  by QUD Technologies, CC-BY: training and evaluation audio and alignments.
- muaalem (obadx/muaalem-model-v3_2), MIT: the teacher model the listener is distilled from.
- quran-transcript (obadx/quran-transcript), MIT: phonemization of the reference.
- k2-fsa icefall (k2-fsa/icefall), Apache-2.0: streaming Zipformer architecture and export.
- Tanzil (https://tanzil.net): the Uthmani Quran text the reference is derived from. Tanzil asks
  that the text be reproduced verbatim and credited to Tanzil, with a link to https://tanzil.net.
- ONNX Runtime (microsoft/onnxruntime), MIT: inference engine (WebAssembly files).
